3. What ace is, and what it is not

ace is an information tool. It reads publicly published university material and, if you choose to give it one, your own degree audit, and it answers questions about them in plain language with a reference to where the answer came from.

ace is not academic advising, and it is not a substitute for your academic adviser. It is not legal, financial or immigration advice. It gives no guarantee about graduation, course registration, eligibility, financial aid, visa status, or any other academic or financial outcome.

Before you drop, add, defer, petition, or rely on a deadline, confirm it with your adviser or the relevant university office. Those decisions, and their consequences, remain yours.
