Construction02

Everything is derived from R, the bowl radius (31 units). Never redraw by eye — use the masters.

The mark · a leans −11° about (5,12)

Bowl stroke 0.71R (22). Stem 22×88, rx 11. Dot r18 at (82,38) — its underside sits on the baseline, y=56.

The wordmark · upright, never leaned

a at 0 · c centre 104 · e centre 180.93 · period centre 248.93. Optical gaps 10 / 4 / 8 — they are not equal on purpose.

Clearspace & minimum size03

Clearspace = R on all four sides

The lean04

The a leans −11°. The box and the period never do. And the wordmark never leans at all — the letter leans when it's alone; the word stands together.

Backgrounds06

The naked mark needs a plain ground with 4.5:1 against the ink. Everything else — photography, colour, pattern, video, anything you don't control — takes the tile. That is what the tile is for, and it is never the wrong answer.

✕ LETTERSPACE
  • Never rebuild it in a font. There is no typeface this is set in — every curve is drawn.
  • Never put the mark inside another shape (circle, badge, ring). The tile is the only container.
  • Never pair the wordmark with the word "ACE" in type. It already says it.
  • Never animate the period as a loading spinner. It marks things that are finished.

Production08

Embroidery

  • Tile only, 25mm minimum. The naked mark's counter fills with thread below that.
  • Two threads: ink + emerald. Satin stitch, 0.4mm pull compensation on the bowl.
  • The dot is a separate colour stop — never blend or gradient it.
  • One-thread alternative: colourway 05, all ink.

Die-cut vinyl / stickers

  • Cut the tile silhouette, 20mm minimum. Bleed 1mm.
  • The bare emerald dot is also an approved standalone die-cut — the only case where the dot appears without the mark.

Screenprint & single-colour

  • Two-colour: ink + #00875A. Pantone match: nearest is Pantone 340 C — confirm on press, never eyeball from a screen.
  • One-colour, engraving, laser, foil, fax: colourway 05. The dot becomes the same ink and the mark still reads.

Motion09

One rule: the period lands last. The letter arrives, settles, and then the dot appears — because the dot means the thing is finished, and finishing happens at the end.

Sequence

Mark fades and settles 0–320ms · dot appears 320–420ms · nothing after. Total under half a second.

Curve

One easing for everything: cubic-bezier(.165,.84,.44,1). No bounce, no overshoot, no spring.

Never

Spinning, pulsing, looping, or using the dot as a progress indicator. A resolved mark does not fidget.

With other logos10

When ACE appears beside a partner mark, separate them with a 1px ink rule and 2R of space on each side. Match optical height, not bounding-box height — our mark is wider than it is tall, so matching boxes makes us look smaller than we are.
