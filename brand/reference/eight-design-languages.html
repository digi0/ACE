THE LAWS — HOLD IN ALL EIGHT

one easing curve carries ~80% of motion
exit pinned 300–400ms; enter scales with mass

spacing quantised to one unit (4px default)

radius: 0 or pill — one decision, global
zero box-shadows on marketing layer
no hue-to-hue gradients, anywhere

mono label tier only over real readouts
density pays for air: ≥5:1 element swing
